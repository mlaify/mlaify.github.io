## The short version

mlaify.io is a static site. We do not run analytics, ad networks, behavioral tracking, or session recording. We do not sell your data. We do not set first-party cookies.

## What we collect

### Technical data (infrastructure level)

When you visit mlaify.io, standard web infrastructure logs may capture:

- IP address (anonymized or truncated at the CDN level)
- Browser and OS type from the HTTP `User-Agent` header
- Pages requested and HTTP status codes
- Referrer URL (if your browser sends one)

This data is processed for **security, performance, and reliability** purposes only. It is not linked to any personal identity and is not retained longer than operationally necessary.

### No analytics, no ads

We run no third-party analytics scripts (no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no Hotjar). We run no advertising or behavioral profiling of any kind.

### Search

The on-site search is powered by [Pagefind](https://pagefind.app) — a fully static, client-side search index. All search processing happens in your browser. No search queries are transmitted to any server.

## Third parties

| Provider | Purpose | Privacy policy |
|---|---|---|

We do not embed third-party social widgets, fonts from remote CDNs (fonts are self-hosted), or tracking pixels.

## Cookies

We do not set first-party cookies. The only browser storage we use is `localStorage` to remember your dark/light mode preference — this value never leaves your device.

## Your rights

Regardless of where you are located, we aim to respect the spirit of:

- **GDPR** (European Union)
- **CCPA / CPRA** (California)
- **Texas TDPSA**

Because we collect no personal data in the traditional sense, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete on our end. If you have a concern, contact us and we will do our best to help.
